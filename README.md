# Lolly Wren Companion

Interactive quantum mechanics demonstrations for **[Schrödinger's Paperwork](https://www.amazon.com)** (*Lolly Wren's Curious Science Adventures*, Book 1) by Q.E.D.

**Live site:** https://lotmus.github.io/lolly-wren-companion/

No account required. No form to complete first. The Ministry of Eventualities would like it on record that it does not endorse this level of accessibility.

## What's here

Eleven demonstrations, each tied to a Physics Note in the book's Appendix:

| Demo | Physics Note | Concept |
|---|---|---|
| [Two Slits](https://lotmus.github.io/lolly-wren-companion/twoslits.html) | 01 | Superposition and interference |
| [The Qubit's Sphere](https://lotmus.github.io/lolly-wren-companion/blochsphere.html) | 01 | Superposition, visualized on the Bloch sphere |
| [Feynman's Glass Trick](https://lotmus.github.io/lolly-wren-companion/glasstrick.html) | 01–02 | Sum-over-paths, QED-style |
| [The Cat in the Box](https://lotmus.github.io/lolly-wren-companion/cat.html) | 02 | The book's namesake thought experiment |
| [A Watched Pot](https://lotmus.github.io/lolly-wren-companion/zeno.html) | 03–04 | The quantum Zeno effect |
| [Through the Wall](https://lotmus.github.io/lolly-wren-companion/tunneling.html) | 06 (bonus) | Quantum tunneling — real closed-form transmission probability, not an approximation |
| [Alice, Bob, and Eve](https://lotmus.github.io/lolly-wren-companion/bb84.html) | 07 | Entanglement and quantum key distribution |
| [Wigner's Friend](https://lotmus.github.io/lolly-wren-companion/wigner.html) | 11 | Two observers, two contradictory-but-correct logbooks |
| [Beat the Classical Limit](https://lotmus.github.io/lolly-wren-companion/bellchsh.html) | 13 | Bell's theorem and the CHSH inequality |
| [Why Quarks Never Travel Alone](https://lotmus.github.io/lolly-wren-companion/confinement.html) | 14 (bonus) | Quark confinement |
| [Maxwell's Demon](https://lotmus.github.io/lolly-wren-companion/maxwell.html) | 16–17 | Entropy, information, and Landauer's Principle |

## Notes

- Everything runs client-side. Nothing is collected, logged, or filed anywhere.
- Several of these demos are shared with the companion repo for *The Quantum World*, the author's non-fiction book on the same material: [quantum-world-simulator](https://github.com/lotmus/quantum-world-simulator).
- Physics is accurate and cited where it matters; the framing is not always serious.

## About the book

*Schrödinger's Paperwork* is a satirical science-fiction novel about a government ministry that tries to measure reality with paperwork, and the quantum mechanics that won't cooperate. Eighteen chapters, eighteen paired physics lectures, no PhD required.

*There is no lid.*
